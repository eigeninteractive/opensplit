THE MARK — SLASHED O
A ring with one diagonal cut — the O of the name, split once. The cut is a knockout, so on a coloured surface it shows that surface through. Clear space is one stroke width on all sides; minimum size 16px.

THEMED · TINTED
One shape, three tints — never a different drawing. mark-mono.svg strokes with currentColor for inlined use (Flutter’s SvgPicture, Android’s themed icon); the pre-stroked mark-on-dark.svg covers contexts that can’t tint.
