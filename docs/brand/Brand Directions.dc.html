Turn 4 · OpenSplit · slashed O · seed colour system
One seed in, everything out.
With Material 3 the only real colour decision is the seed — every role, both brightnesses and all three contrast levels are generated from it, and dynamic colour can override it entirely on Android. So this turn is five seeds (4a–4e) shown as what they actually produce, then the parts a seed can’t decide: what survives Material You, and the exact literals the Flutter, Android and web builds each need.

WHAT SURVIVES MATERIAL YOU
On Android 12+ the user’s wallpaper replaces your seed. Turn 3 · OpenSplit · mark exploration
Fourteen marks, one rule.
Every mark below is one shape at one stroke weight, so it survives a 16px favicon, a single-colour Android 13 themed icon, and a laser-cut sticker. Shown in the 2a teal for comparison — any of them drops into either theme. Call out ids like 3a; the shortlist at the bottom shows my three at real sizes.
3a · SPLIT O
The current 2a mark. Literal, calm, unmistakably an O.
3b · SLIPPED O
Cut and offset — a split that doesn’t quite line up. More motion.
3c · TWO HALVES
Wide gap: reads as two parties facing each other, not one broken ring.
3d · OPEN RING
A single notch at 12 o’clock. “Open” before “split”. Quietest of the set.
3e · SPLIT SQUIRCLE
Same idea on M3’s own corner radius — sits natively inside the icon mask.
3f · CUT COIN
Solid, two tones. Heaviest presence on a homescreen; needs a second colour.
3g · EQUALS
Settling up = making both sides equal. Busy under 24px.
3h · SHARED
Two rings overlapping — the shared amount is the intersection.
3i · SHARES
Four unequal shares. Honest about uneven splits; risks reading as a chart app.
3j · SLASH
Two people, one divider. Pairs with the open/split wordmark in 2b.
3k · QUARTERS
A bill quartered. Simple and scalable, but generic — many apps own this.
3l · SPLIT O + DOT
3a with a centre point — the amount at the middle of the split.

3m · BRACKET SLASH
Purely typographic, no drawing to maintain. Very 2b; reads as a dev tool.
3n · SLASHED O

The mark is an O split once — the name drawn, not illustrated. One shape, one stroke weight, legible at 16px, and it doubles as the monochrome themed-icon layer Android 13+ asks for.
Dynamic colour can stay on: because meaning maps to roles rather than hex values, a Material You wallpaper theme still reads correctly.
