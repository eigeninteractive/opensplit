Promises, written down.

OpenSplit exists because splitting a bill shouldn't come with a daily limit or an upsell. These are commitments, not aspirations, published so they can be held against us.

Read the source All the principles

  1. Logging an expense is never limited.

    No daily caps, no expense limits, no "you've used 3 of your 4 free entries". It stays free for everyone.

  2. No ads, no analytics, no data sale.

    Your spending says where you go and who with. It is not a dataset, and nothing in the app watches what you do.

  3. No venture funding.

    Money that demands growth is what turns a free app into a paywalled one. OpenSplit doesn't take it.

  4. Licensed AGPL-3.0.

    Nobody can take the code, close it, and run it as their own service. Changes to a hosted copy have to be shared.

  5. Paid only for convenience, if ever.

    Paying might one day make something easier. Not paying will never make anything impossible.

  6. You can always leave with everything.

    Your groups are already on your device in plain SQLite, and export to CSV whenever you ask.

  7. The app outlives the project.

    If the servers disappeared tomorrow, the app would keep working with what it has.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Adding an expense is never limited, and there are no ads. If OpenSplit ever charges for anything, it will be for convenience, like a hosted extra, and never for something the app does today.

Where does my data live?

On your device first. The app keeps your groups in a local database and does every calculation there; the server stores the rows it needs to sync them between the people in a group. There is no analytics SDK in the app. The privacy policy has the details.

Who makes OpenSplit?

EigenInteractive, without venture funding and in the open. The source is on GitHub under the AGPL-3.0, and bug reports and ideas are welcome there.
