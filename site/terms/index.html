What OpenSplit is

OpenSplit is a tool for recording shared expenses and working out who owes whom. It is free to use. Recording an expense is never limited, capped or gated, there are no adverts, and there is nothing to buy.

What OpenSplit is not

OpenSplit does not handle money. It is not a bank, a wallet, a payment processor or a money transmitter. It never holds, moves, transfers or settles funds. If you add a UPI ID, the app can hand the details to your own payment app — the payment happens entirely between you, that app and your bank, and OpenSplit is not part of it and does not know whether it happened.

Balances shown in the app are a calculation over what you and the people in your groups typed in. They are not a statement of account, not legal or financial advice, and not evidence of a debt. Sorting out what anyone actually owes anyone is between you.

Remember that a shared group is shared. Expenses you add are visible to everyone in that group, and stay part of that group's record even after you leave or delete your account.
